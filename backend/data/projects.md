# Selected Research and Technical Projects

## M.Tech thesis: generalized downlink and uplink NOMA framework

**Indian Institute of Technology Ropar | July 2025–May 2026**

Shashi studied multi-user power-domain NOMA for future-generation wireless systems.
He derived analytical performance expressions and built MATLAB Monte Carlo simulations
for bit-error-rate and throughput analysis. The work considered two-to-four-user and
generalized N-user uplink and downlink cases under flat Rayleigh fading, with power
allocation, distance-based user ordering, interference, SIC depth, SIC-error modelling,
oversampling, and NOMA-versus-OMA throughput comparison.

This is Shashi's principal completed research project and demonstrates mathematical
derivation, probabilistic channel modelling, numerical simulation, and validation of
analytical results. A related manuscript is under internal faculty review before
planned submission to an IEEE Transactions journal; it is not under IEEE peer review.

## Echo cancellation using an NLMS adaptive filter

**January–May 2025**

Implemented and evaluated an echo-cancellation model in MATLAB using the normalized
least-mean-squares adaptive-filter algorithm. The project focused on adaptive-filter
behaviour and signal-processing analysis.
